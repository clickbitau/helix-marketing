Getting Started

What Helix is, what you need, and how the pieces fit together.

What is Helix?
Three products, one system: Helix Server streams your local collection, Helix plays movies and series, Helix Music plays audio. Start with the Server — the apps connect to it.
Do I need all three apps?
Only Helix Server is required — it's the hub. Then install Helix for video, Helix Music for audio, or both. Use whichever apps fit your screens.
Do I need to be technical to set it up?
No. Install Helix Server on any always-on computer or NAS, point it at your media folders, and install the apps on your devices. Indexing, metadata, and artwork are handled automatically.
Does Helix require a subscription?
No subscriptions. Helix runs on hardware you own, with your own media — grab the latest release from the downloads page and it's yours.
How is Helix different from streaming services?
Streaming services rent you a catalog that changes monthly. Helix streams media you own, from hardware you own — nothing disappears, nothing gets compressed, no monthly fee.

Apps & Devices

Where Helix runs and how your screens stay in sync.

What devices does Helix support?
Windows, macOS, and Linux on desktop; Android and iOS on mobile; Android TV in the living room; CarPlay and Android Auto for music on the road.
Can I cast to my TV?
Yes — the mobile app casts to the big screen in one tap, and the dedicated Android TV app gives you a full 10-foot interface designed for the couch.
Does my progress sync across devices?
Yes. Pause on the TV, resume on your phone. Watch progress, playlists, and libraries stay in sync through your Helix Server.
What quality does playback support?
Helix direct-plays your files in original quality — up to 4K Dolby Vision and HDR video, and bit-perfect lossless audio with gapless output in Helix Music.

Privacy & Ownership

Where your media lives and who can see it.

Is my media collection private?
Yes. Your files stay on your hardware and stream over your own network. Helix is built around sovereign media architecture — your library and viewing habits belong to you.
Where are my files stored?
Exactly where you put them — your drives, your NAS, your machine. Helix indexes them in place; nothing is uploaded to a cloud.
Can my family use it too?
Yes. Multi-user profiles give everyone their own library, watch progress, and permissions — all managed from your Helix Server.
