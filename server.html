Core Capabilities

Everything Your Server Needs.

Six powerful pillars engineered to make media management effortless.

Intelligent Library Scanning

Automatically parses, organizes, and deduplicates your Movies, TV Shows, and Music collections. Drop in your drives and let the engine handle the rest -- no manual tagging required.

Dynamic Stream Optimization

Real-time, on-demand video conversion ensures smooth playback on every screen. Whether you are streaming to a flagship phone or a 4K television, Helix adapts automatically.

Universal Source Aggregation

Seamlessly indexes media from diverse local and web-based sources into a single, unified library. One search, one interface -- every piece of content at your fingertips.

Rich Metadata Integration

Automatically pulls gorgeous high-resolution posters, backdrops, and detailed cast information. Your library looks as premium as any major streaming platform.

Secure Multi-User Profiles

Keep your household organized with PIN-protected profiles. Each family member gets their own personalized recommendations, watch history, and content restrictions.

Two-Way Trakt.tv Sync

Sync your watch history, ratings, and watchlists bidirectionally across all your devices. Switch platforms without losing a single episode of progress.
